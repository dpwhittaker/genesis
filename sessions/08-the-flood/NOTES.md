## Threads Session 7 hands forward

- **Noah as the answer to the cursed ground.** Session 7 ends on 5:29 — nine
  generations under the curse of 3:17 / 4:11–12, and Lamech naming his son
  *Rest*. Genesis 9:20, *"Noah, a man of the soil, began to plant a
  vineyard,"* is the payoff: the ground yields again after the flood. Session
  7 promised the class this lands here. *Genesis Rabbah* 25:2 (on the Session
  7 rabbinic sheet) has the ox and the furrow obeying the plough again, and
  the two other *n-w-ḥ* landings — the ark *rests* (8:4), the *restful*
  aroma (8:21).
- **Methuselah dies in the flood year** (MT arithmetic: 187 + 182 + 600 =
  969). Session 7 made the point; don't repeat it, but it is available.
- **Noah "walked with God"** (6:9) — only he and Enoch get the phrase. Session
  7's passage sheet, note 4, has the grammar (hithpael, *ʾet* not *lipnê*).
- **Naamah = Noah's wife** (*Genesis Rabbah* 23:3) — the two genealogies
  married. Already quoted on the Session 7 rabbinic sheet.
- **Genesis 6:3, "120 years"** — Session 7 read it as the cliff in the
  lifespan curve (the King List's post-flood collapse). *Genesis Rabbah* 26:2
  reads it as the age of liability, which is why Noah fathers at 500.
- **The Watchers / sons of God (6:1–4).** *Jubilees* 4:15 puts the angels'
  descent in Jared's days (a pun on *yārad*) and says they came *to instruct*
  — the *apkallu* job. *1 Enoch* 6–16 is the full story; Session 7 quoted 12
  and 14–15 on the earliest-readings sheet. Josephus 1.3.1 ("the acts of
  those whom the Grecians call Giants") is already on that sheet too.
- **Sippar / the buried tablets.** Berossus has Xisuthrus bury "the entire
  book" at Sippar before the flood so that knowledge survives; Josephus gives
  Seth's line the same motif (pillars of brick and stone, *Ant.* 1.2.3). Both
  are on the Session 7 sheets if the flood session wants the "learning
  preserved through the flood" theme.

## ANE flood texts

- ***Atrahasis*** is **already in the repo** from Session 1:
  [`sessions/01-the-neighbors-stories/texts/atrahasis.md`](../01-the-neighbors-stories/texts/atrahasis.md)
  (George Smith 1876, public domain) — Part Two covers famine and flood.
- ***Gilgamesh* XI** — check whether Session 1 has a licensed text; it may
  not. Public-domain options: George Smith, *The Chaldean Account of Genesis*
  (1876); R. Campbell Thompson, *The Epic of Gilgamish* (1928/1930) — Thompson
  died 1941, so his 1928 text is PD in life+70 jurisdictions and pre-1929 in
  the US. Prefer Thompson if usable.
- **Sumerian flood story / Eridu Genesis** (ETCSL 1.7.4) — already credited
  on Session 1's Sumerian sheet (translation adapted by Gábor Zólyomi, CC
  BY-NC-SA). Ziusudra of Šuruppag is the hero, which ties straight back to
  the King List (Ubara-Tutu of Šuruppag is the last king before the flood).
- **Berossus's flood** (Xisuthrus) — Bedrosian's public-domain Eusebius
  translation, already cited on the Session 7 Sumerian sheet; the flood
  paragraph (Kronos in a dream, the birds released three times) is on the
  same page.
